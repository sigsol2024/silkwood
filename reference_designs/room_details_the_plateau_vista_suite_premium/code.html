About the Room

Crafted from the landscape.

The Plateau Vista is an exercise in restraint and material honesty. We sourced native granite from the surrounding hills to anchor the space, balanced by the warmth of reclaimed mahogany.

Every textile, from the hand-loomed linen drapery to the wool rugs, has been chosen to mirror the organic textures of the highland plateau. It is a space designed not just for shelter, but for profound connection to the environment.

Wake up to the mist rolling over the Shere Hills from your private balcony, and end the day in a freestanding soaking tub that blurs the line between indoors and out. The architecture defers entirely to the view.
