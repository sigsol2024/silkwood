Our Heritage

Est. 1938 — Plateau State

A Sanctuary in the Shere Hills

Forged from the very stone of the Plateau, The Hill Station was conceived in 1938 as a retreat from the tropical heat. It quickly became the nexus of colonial administration and high society, a place where the cool highland air met uncompromising hospitality.

Today, we preserve that indelible legacy. Every archway and timber whispers stories of a bygone era, thoughtfully restored to offer a modern sanctuary that honors its deeply rooted history.

Our Philosophy

Elevated Living,
Grounded Culture.

We believe true luxury is a reflection of its environment. Our commitment to the Plateau culture extends beyond aesthetics; it is woven into the fabric of our service. From locally sourced culinary ingredients that celebrate the region's fertile soil, to the integration of indigenous artisanal crafts within our suites, we offer an experience that is authentically, unapologetically Jos.
