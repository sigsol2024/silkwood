A refined stay in Ikeja GRA

Silkwood Hotel is a four-star stay in Ikeja GRA for business and leisure travellers. There are 106 rooms across five types: Classic, Deluxe, Premium, Executive, and Diplomatic Suite.

About 15 minutes from the airport, and close to Ikeja City Mall and Ikeja Secretariat. Dining, a pool, gym, Strands Spa, and a meeting room for up to 60 guests sit on property.
