Your Home Away From Home

Silkwood Hotel is a four-star stay in Ikeja GRA for business and leisure travellers. About 15 minutes from the airport, and close to Ikeja City Mall and Ikeja Secretariat.

There are 106 rooms across five types, plus a restaurant, bar, coffee shop, pool, Strands Bar, gym, and Strands Spa. Check-in is from 14:00; check-out is by 11:00.
